Repair Explanation

Problem: grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 400px) forces at least 1248px including gaps.

Fix: Replace fixed columns with repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 260px), 1fr)).

Verification: Resize to 375px, 768px, and 1440px. The page should have no horizontal overflow. Only the intentionally broken example has an internal scrollbar.
